# Life in Weeks

A simple, beautiful visualization tool to help you reflect on the passage of time by showing your life in weeks.

## Features

- 📊 Visual grid showing weeks lived vs. weeks remaining
- 📈 Detailed life statistics (heartbeats, breaths, days, etc.)
- 🌍 Societal context (population growth, people met)
- 🌌 Cosmic perspective (Earth's journey through space)
- 🌿 Natural world insights
- 💾 Export your visualization as an HTML file
- 🌐 Multi-language support (English, Spanish - more coming)

## Demo

[Live Demo](https://veluribharath.github.io/lifegrid)

## Usage

Simply visit the site, enter your birthdate, and explore your life visualized in weeks.

## Privacy

All calculations happen in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
